Explore Sapao

A slower, more meaningful stretch of coastline

Nestled along the serene coastline of Guiuan, Eastern Samar, Barangay Sapao offers a peaceful blend of natural beauty, cultural heritage, and warm community life. From its sandy shorelines to its local landmarks, Sapao welcomes visitors looking for a slower, more meaningful travel experience.

Follow the local trails, enjoy the sea breeze, and learn the stories behind each destination — most still cared for by the families and residents who call this shoreline home.

Where to go in Sapao

Five places, five ways to know Sapao

Craft & Heritage

Sitio San Roque

Home to Sapao's blacksmiths, coconut farmers, and shell and scabbard artisans — traditions passed down through generations and celebrated every August 20th at the Sr. San Roque fiesta.

Meet the Panday, the Paraglukad, and the shell and scabbard crafters, and see the fiesta that brings the whole sitio together each year.

Explore Sitio San Roque

Coastal Escape

Coca Beach

One of Sapao's quieter coastal treasures, known for its open-air cottages, calm atmosphere, and easy access from the barangay — inviting for families or couples after a simple beach day away from the town center.

According to local stories, the beach became a community destination after the Sison family opened the area for public use, turning a private family space into a welcoming spot for gatherings and rest.

Coastal Escape

Dumpao Beach

A calm and spacious shoreline that attracts visitors seeking a quiet day by the sea. Its simple cottages, open-air setting, and peaceful atmosphere suit family trips, beach picnics, and community gatherings.

Local stories remember the area as a place shaped by typhoons, resilience, and rebuilding — even after Typhoon Yolanda, Dumpao remained a meaningful place for residents and visitors alike.

Weather Station

PAGASA-DOST

A practical and important facility for the barangay and nearby communities. Its elevated location helps monitor changing weather conditions and support disaster preparedness during strong winds, typhoons, and heavy rain.

The station also educates residents about weather alerts, rainfall advisories, and climate updates that help fisherfolk, farmers, and families plan their days safely.

Parish & Community

Sta. Cruz Parish

The spiritual heart of Barangay Sapao, bringing residents together through worship, festivals, service, and shared tradition. Its annual fiesta is one of the most beloved community events, drawing families and visitors from near and far.

Beyond worship, the parish is known for its youth ministry and service programs, keeping the community connected through faith, culture, and mutual care.

About Barangay Sapao

Coastal life, kept by the people who live it

Barangay Sapao is a coastal community in Guiuan, Eastern Samar, known for its warm people, strong community ties, and natural attractions. Its shoreline, local landmarks, and shared traditions make it a place worth visiting for travelers who want more than just a beach stop.

Sapao continues to grow through the work of local officials, residents, and community organizations — from livelihood and public services to cultural events and tourism initiatives — while staying focused on preserving its identity and heritage.

Plan your visit

A short ride from Guiuan town proper

From Guiuan town proper, Sapao is a short ride away and makes an easy day trip for travelers who want to experience the coast and the local culture of Eastern Samar. Most destinations are reached by tricycle along the barangay road, followed by a short walk to the shore.

Since several sites are still developing as tourism spots, it's best to ask around the barangay or coordinate with the barangay office for current access and hours.

Get in touch

Planning a trip to Sapao?

If you are planning a visit or would like more information about Sapao, the barangay office is the best place to start — they can point you to the latest cottage availability, fiesta dates, and local Facebook updates.
